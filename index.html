Money Management Tips & Best Practices

1. Safe-to-Spend Rule

Checking Balance minus Unpaid Bills, Savings Commitments, and Emergency Buffer.

2. Daily Allowance Pace

Safe-to-Spend divided by days remaining until payday. Stay under daily burn.

3. Emergency Buffer

Keep $250–$500 untouched in checking to protect against unexpected fees.

4. 24-Hour Rule

If a simulated purchase drops allowance below $10/day, wait 24 hours before buying.

Quick start: add a ticker and shares, then save & refresh.
Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Enter the ticker (like NVDA), shares, and optional average cost.
  2. Press Save holding & refresh next to that investment. The window stays open and SpendWise requests a fresh available quote.
  3. Use + Add investment for your next stock, ETF, or crypto holding. Repeat until you're finished.
  4. Enter uninvested cash only below, if any. Your account estimate is calculated from your saved investments and cash.
  5. Press Save Account at the bottom to finish.

Market prices refresh about every 60 seconds while this page is open; they may be delayed. SpendWise is not connected to your investment platform; it calculates from the shares and prices you enter or refresh.
