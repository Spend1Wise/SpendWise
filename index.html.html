Student Cash Flow Guidelines & Best Practices

1. Safe-to-Spend Rule

Checking Balance minus Unpaid Bills, Savings Commitments, and Emergency Buffer.

2. Daily Allowance Pace

Safe-to-Spend divided by days remaining until payday. Stay under daily burn.

3. Emergency Buffer

Keep $250–$500 untouched in checking to protect against unexpected fees.

4. 24-Hour Rule

If a simulated purchase drops allowance below $10/day, wait 24 hours before buying.

Privacy & Local Storage Information

Zero-Server Privacy Guarantee: SpendWise does not require accounts, bank logins, credit card info, or server storage. All financial inputs stay 100% inside your browser's local storage (localStorage).

Local Device Notice: Because data is stored locally in this browser, clearing browser history or cache will reset your figures. Anyone with physical access to this browser may view saved entries on this device.

Embedded Google Sites Note: If third-party storage partitioning is blocked by your browser, SpendWise automatically falls back to secure session storage so calculations work cleanly without crashing.
